Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Effective immediately

1. Our Commitment to Health Data Privacy

At FitPulse, we believe your fitness, nutrition, and metabolic metrics are deeply personal. We never sell, rent, or monetize your health data to third-party data brokers or advertisers. FitPulse is funded transparently through optional Premium memberships.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, and cryptographically salted password hashes (never plaintext).
  • Health & Biometrics: Age, height, body weight, goal weight, gender, dietary preferences, and target macro goals.
  • Tracking Logs: Daily food items, hydration intake, sleep duration, step counts, habit checks, and completed workouts.
  • Support & Enquiries: Inquiries and tickets submitted to our technical support desk.

3. How Your Data is Used

Your data is utilized solely to power your personalized fitness intelligence:

  • To calculate accurate Basal Metabolic Rates (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).
  • To generate context-aware guidance via your personalized AI Coach.
  • To display historical streak timelines and progress analytics.

4. Security & Storage Architecture

We enforce enterprise-grade security standards across our application:

  • Password Hashing: Multi-round scrypt and bcrypt cryptographic key derivation with per-user salt.
  • Session Security: Cryptographically random 256-bit session tokens stored with HttpOnly, SameSite protection.
  • Access Control: Strict user-isolation SQL parameterization preventing unauthorized cross-tenant data access.
  • Brute Force Prevention: Active IP and credential rate-limiting guards against automated attacks.

5. Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). FitPulse servers never store, process, or view your complete credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

6. Your Rights & Data Portability

You maintain absolute ownership of your data:

  • Data Export: You may request a complete CSV/JSON export of all your logged metrics.
  • Account Deletion: You can request full erasure of your account and all associated logs by submitting a ticket through our Support Center.

7. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For privacy inquiries, audit verification, or data subject requests, email privacy@fitpulse.app or reach out through our Contact Page.
