Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Effective immediately

1. Overview & Fair Billing Commitment

At FitPulse, we strive to deliver an exceptional digital fitness experience. We believe in complete transparency and customer fairness. This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms governing cancellations, renewals, and refunds for FitPulse Premium membership subscriptions purchased on our platform.

2. Subscription Cancellation

You have full freedom to cancel your subscription at any time without questions or penalties:

  • Self-Service Cancellation: You can cancel your membership anytime directly from your account by navigating to Subscription Settings and clicking "Cancel Subscription".
  • Retained Access: When you cancel, your Premium access remains active through the end of your current prepaid billing period. Your account will not be billed for subsequent cycles.
  • Downgrade to Free Tier: Upon expiration of the prepaid term, your account reverts automatically to our permanent Free tier. Your historical logs, weight entries, and workout history remain preserved.

3. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your fitness journey. We offer a 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee on all first-time Premium purchases:

  • Eligibility Window: If you are dissatisfied with FitPulse for any reason, you may request a 100% full refund within 7 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase date.
  • Annual Plans: For annual subscriptions (12 months), if a refund request is made after the 7-day initial window but within 30 days due to verified technical inability to access the service, a pro-rated refund minus payment gateway transaction fees may be issued at management discretion.
  • Duplicate Charges: In the event of an accidental double charge caused by network timeouts or duplicate transactions, the duplicate payment is refunded automatically and immediately upon identification.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Requests submitted after the 7-day money-back guarantee window has elapsed.
  • Accounts terminated or suspended due to violation of our Terms of Service (e.g. automated abuse, payment fraud, or reverse engineering).
  • Promotional coupons or discount credits, which hold no cash surrender value.

5. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes

When a refund is approved by our billing team:

  • Processing Window: Refunds are initiated within 24 to 48 hours of verification.
  • Bank Credit Timeframe: Depending on your financial institution or UPI app, funds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Payment Mode: All refunds are issued strictly to the original source payment method (Credit/Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking, or Wallet) used during checkout via our verified payment gateway (Razorpay / Stripe). We cannot issue refunds in cash or to third-party bank accounts.

6. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, follow these simple steps:

  1. Open our Help & Support Desk or email our billing desk at billing@fitpulse.app.
  2. Provide your registered account email and your Order ID (found in your email receipt or Billing History).
  3. Briefly state your reason for requesting a refund. Our support team will confirm receipt within 24 hours.
