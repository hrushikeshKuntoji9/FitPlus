How It Works

The Daily Health Pulse (0–100)

Your score isn't a vanity metric — it is an algorithmic synthesis of your daily biological inputs:

  • 25% Calorie Balance — Staying within 10% of your metabolic target.
  • 20% Protein Adequacy — Meeting 1.6–2.0g per kg body mass for lean mass preservation.
  • 20% Hydration — Meeting minimum 3L daily cellular fluid replenishment.
  • 15% NEAT & Movement — Hitting 10,000 steps of non-exercise activity.
  • 10% Restorative Sleep — Minimum 7–8 hours of quality recovery.
  • 10% Training Stimulus — Completing structured resistance or cardio workout.
